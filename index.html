Advertising

This game is supported by Google AdMob and serves rewarded video ads only.

A rewarded ad is one you choose to watch in exchange for something — for example doubling your gold or continuing after a defeat. We never show an ad that interrupts you without warning, and there are no interstitials in this game.

  • AdMob uses the Advertising ID (GAID) to serve and measure advertising.
  • Opt out of personalised ads at Google Ads Settings, or enable Limit Ad Tracking on your device.
  • Opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. See Google's partner sites policy.

Children

Bricks Breaker is suitable for all ages and is intended to be family-friendly.

  • The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
  • The game is configured for child-directed treatment, so the advertising platform does not serve interest-based advertising to this app.
  • We do not use behavioural advertising to build a profile of a child.
  • If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

  • Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the app. We hold no copy.
  • Advertising data is retained and managed by Google under Google's own retention policies. We do not store advertising data ourselves.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your game data, most requests concern advertising data held by Google — use the opt-out links above, or contact us and we will point you to the right authority.

To erase everything this app holds on your device: Settings → Apps → Bricks Breaker → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app.

Security

All game data is local to your device. We do not transmit or store game data on our servers, because we do not operate game servers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version published at this same URL before the change takes effect.
