# Privacy Policy — Cannon Raid Bricks Breaker

**Developer:** AZ Group (AZ GP Games)
**Contact:** azgroup.games@gmail.com
**Effective date:** 25 September 2026

## Summary

Cannon Raid Bricks Breaker is an offline arcade game. Your game progress, coins, weapons and
settings are stored **only on your device** and are never uploaded to us. We do
not have user accounts, and we do not operate game servers.

The only data that leaves your device is advertising data handled by **Google
AdMob**, which is used to show you ads.

## Data we collect

| Data | Why | Shared with |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Advertising ID (GAID) | To show and measure ads | Google AdMob |
| Advertising data (interests, ad interactions) | Ad delivery and measurement | Google AdMob |
| App performance and crash data | To find and fix bugs | Google AdMob, Google Play Console |
| Device model, OS version, app version | Compatibility and ad targeting | Google AdMob |

### Data stored on your device only (never uploaded)

Coins, unlocked cannons, upgrade levels, mission progress, completed missions
and settings. This data lives in local storage on your device. **We cannot see
it, and we cannot recover it if you uninstall the app.**

## Advertising

This game is supported by **Google AdMob** and serves **rewarded video ads only**.

A rewarded ad is one you choose to watch in exchange for something — for example
doubling your gold or continuing after a defeat. We never show an ad that
interrupts you without warning, and there are no interstitials in this game.

- AdMob uses the Advertising ID (GAID) to serve and measure advertising.
- You can opt out of personalised ads at
  [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or by enabling
  **Limit Ad Tracking** on your device.
- You can opt out of third-party vendor cookies at
  [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads
based on your visits to this and other sites. See Google's
[partner sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Third-party SDKs

| SDK | Purpose | Data touched |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) | Rewarded advertising | Advertising ID, ad data, device info |

This is the only third-party SDK included in the published Android build. It was
verified against the shipped app manifest, which declares only the AdMob plugin
and the permissions `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `VIBRATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`
and `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`.

## Children

Cannon Raid Bricks Breaker is suitable for **all ages** and is intended to be family-friendly.

- The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
- The game is configured for **child-directed treatment**, which means the
  advertising platform does not serve interest-based advertising to this app.
- We do not use behavioural advertising to build a profile of a child.
- If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the
  address above and we will delete it.

## Data retention and deletion

- **Local game data** is deleted when you uninstall the app. We hold no copy.
- **Advertising data** is retained and managed by Google under Google's own
  retention policies. We do not store advertising data ourselves.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or
port your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

Because we do not hold your game data, most requests concern advertising data held
by Google — use the opt-out links above, or contact us and we will point you to
the right authority.

To erase everything this app holds on your device: **Settings → Apps →
Cannon Raid Bricks Breaker → Clear data**, or simply uninstall the app.

## Security

All game data is local to your device. We do not transmit or store game data on
our servers, because we do not operate game servers.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new
version published at this same URL before the change takes effect.
